What IRIS can and cannot do

  • CAN: read a public transaction hash and show what it does on chain; walk checklists on your phone for unknown devices and apps.
  • CANNOT: recover funds; reverse a transaction; say something is safe; verify who called, emailed, or messaged you.
  • Go to your exchange, bank, or police if money moved without your consent, someone impersonates Apple / bank / support, or you fear for your safety.
Before you change passwords Secure the account from a clean device first. Changing a password or removing an unknown device from a compromised phone can hand over the new password or confirm the attacker still has access.
About pasting a hash A hash check shows what a transaction does. That is not the same as safe. IRIS cannot tell you to sign, connect a wallet, or approve.
